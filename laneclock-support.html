LaneClock Support

Effective date: November 11, 2026.

Time a swim group from one screen. Choose a 25 m or 50 m pool, enter swimmer names, and start everyone together. Tap a swimmer's card after each pool length. Undo reverses the most recent action. End a set to retain partial results, then open Training history.

Free and full version

The free version supports up to four swimmers and 100/200 m targets. Timing, undo, interruption recovery, access to all saved records, and JSON backup/restore remain available without purchase. The optional one-time full-version unlock adds five to eight swimmers, 400/800 m targets, history search and CSV export. There is no subscription. Apple displays your local price before purchase.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase, open Prepare training, and select Restore purchases. A network connection may be required for Apple to verify the purchase. If a purchase is still pending, wait for Apple's confirmation rather than purchasing again.

Backgrounding and interruption

Timing continues through backgrounding or locking while the app process remains alive. If the app is terminated or restarted, the unfinished set is marked interrupted at its last saved checkpoint. LaneClock does not invent elapsed time after termination. Review the saved splits before starting a new set.

Keep a backup

Export a JSON backup before changing devices or deleting the app. Keep the file in a location you control. Import a compatible LaneClock JSON backup when no training set is active. CSV is for viewing or sharing results, not restoring training history. Exported files may include swimmer names and results, so choose recipients carefully.

Languages

English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese are supported. The app follows your iOS preferred languages. Existing swimmer names are not translated.

Safety and limitations

LaneClock is a manual training aid, not an official competition timer, a medical device or a water-safety monitor. Test before training, keep a backup stopwatch ready, and protect your device from water.
