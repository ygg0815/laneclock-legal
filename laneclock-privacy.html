LaneClock Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026.

LaneClock is operated by Wei Ye. Contact: 2603462296@qq.com.

Training data

Swimmer names, training settings, recorded splits and training history are stored locally on your device. LaneClock does not require an account and does not operate a developer backend, advertising or analytics service. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time in-app purchase. LaneClock uses StoreKit to verify purchase entitlement and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple's services and privacy policies apply to payment processing.

Backups and sharing

Device backups may include app data according to your device settings. You may export JSON backups and, with the full version, CSV records. Exported files can contain swimmer names and training results. They are shared only through the destination you choose. Other apps or storage providers handle files you send to them under their own policies. LaneClock does not automatically upload training records to us.

Data control

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app through iOS. Offloading an app may retain its data. Exported files and device backups are separate copies: manage or delete them in the apps or backup services where you saved them. Export a backup before deleting the app if you want to keep your history.

Support

If you email us, your message and any information you include are received through email to respond to your request. Do not send payment details or unnecessary personal information. Use swimmer nicknames when possible.

Changes

This page will be updated when the app's data practices change.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web connection information to GitHub as the hosting provider. This is separate from training data in the app. The page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or embedded third-party content.
